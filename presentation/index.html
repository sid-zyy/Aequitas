What we built

A second pair of eyes that writes down only what it sees.

It watches CCTV, police bodycams and citizen uploads.

It writes a plain summary of every four seconds and flags possible incidents.

An officer confirms or dismisses each one, and every decision is logged.

Anyone with access can ask questions and gets answers that point to the exact clip and minute.

Runs on one laptop, offline

Footage never leaves the station. Nothing to leak and no cloud bill.

Describes, never identifies

No face recognition. No sorting people by religion, caste, party, flags or clothes.

A protest is not an incident

A peaceful gathering at Jantar Mantar is a right. Aequitas never flags it.

A human decides

The AI flags. An officer confirms. The record shows who decided what.

Evidence you can check

Every event keeps its key frame, timestamp, camera and a file hash.

Latency is judged hardest, so we took the fastest model that fits. Then we closed the accuracy gap with a second signal rather than a bigger model, and taught E2B from 12B (incidents found 20 % → 60 % on the same dataset).

Fixing accuracy · a second signal

15 % isn't enough. So it never decides alone.

Gemma described the faculty fights as people standing and sitting. A small video model (r3d-18, trained on 8,000 clips) watches motion instead. It votes with Gemma, and on its own it needs a score of 0.9. It runs on the CPU in 90 ms, beside the GPU, so it adds almost nothing to the wait.

0 → 6 / 6faculty clips flagged as violent: Gemma's own labels vs the violence model (every clip scored 0.96 or more)
77 %of fights caught on 300 unseen real CCTV clips, with 14 % false alarms
20 → 60 %incidents found after fine-tuning E2B on 12B's answers (same dataset). Tested, not in the live demo yet: its 4-bit export failed.
Quantization · 4-bit vs 8-bit

On a 6 GB GPU, only 4-bit fits.

PrecisionGemma 4 E2B weightsFits our 6 GB?Accuracy, 12B on GSM8KSpeed vs BF16
BF16 (full)~10 GBNo0.9641.00×
8-bit (FP8)~5 GBToo tight with the image encoder and cache0.9671.10×
4-bit (Q4_K_M)used3.5 GBYes0.961 *–

Published benchmarks show 8-bit and 4-bit cost almost no accuracy on Gemma 4 (within 0.3 points of full precision). Google's own 4-bit release cuts memory by about 72 %. For us the choice was made by memory: 4-bit is the only version that runs on the laptop.

Accuracy and speed: AxionML Gemma-4-12B FP8 / NVFP4 model cards (GSM8K, one Blackwell GPU). * NVFP4, a different 4-bit format from ours · memory: Google blog, Gemma 4 QAT

Then we made it faster.

Improvement 1 · Scene gate (SigLIP)

Don't ask the big model about an empty road.

A small image–text model compares each window with descriptions of normal and incident scenes in 44 ms. Calm street scenes skip Gemma. It never skips the first window of a camera, never when the violence model is worried, and Gemma still looks at least every 30 s.

Decoding is 1.3× faster for summaries and 1.85× for chat. A window gets only 9.5 % faster, because most of its time goes to reading the image. Chat answers get 22 % faster, and no label changed in a way that matters (0 / 33 false alarms at every setting).
